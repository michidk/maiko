Maiko

Play The Elder Scrolls Adventures: Redguard natively on Windows, Linux and macOS.

Requires your own copy of Redguard. Your executable stays on this device.

  • NativeRuns as a normal program on your system. No DOSBox.
  • PrivateYour RGFX.EXE is turned into the game right here in your browser. Nothing is uploaded.
  • OriginalThe original menus, movies, music and saves, plus gamepad support.

Once your ZIP is ready

      
  1. Extract the ZIP into its own folder, anywhere you can write to. Not into your game folder.
  2. Double-click redguard-native.exe. Maiko looks for your game on its own, in the usual GOG folders among others. If it cannot find it, it asks once for the folder that contains RGFX.EXE and remembers your choice.
  3. The original book menu opens. Choose New Game or Continue.

      
  1. Extract the ZIP into its own folder, anywhere you can write to. Not into your game folder.
  2. Open a terminal in that folder and run ./redguard-native. Maiko looks for your game on its own; if it cannot find it, it asks in the terminal for the folder that contains RGFX.EXE and remembers your choice. You can also pass it with --game-dir /path/to/Redguard.
  3. The original book menu opens. Choose New Game or Continue.

      
Options such as fullscreen are listed in the README. Prefer the terminal? Use the Rust command-line tool.

    
Once your Mac ZIP is ready
  1. Extract the ZIP into its own folder.
  2. Open redguard-native. Select your owned Redguard installation if asked. The ZIP contains the runtime; you supply the game files.
  3. If macOS blocks the download, open Terminal and remove quarantine from the extracted folder:
    xattr -dr com.apple.quarantine "$HOME/Downloads/maiko-macos-universal"
    Adjust the path if you extracted it elsewhere, then open redguard-native again.
Read the Mac launch guide for details.

    
Saves, settings and logs

      
Every version keeps your saves and settings in a user-data folder and writes a log of each start. Your game folder is never changed. If something goes wrong, attach the newest log to an issue.
