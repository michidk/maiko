Play The Elder Scrolls Adventures: Redguard natively on Windows or Linux, without DOSBox.

Requires your own copy of Redguard. Your executable stays on this device.

Build your package

Where will you play?

Select RGFX.EXE from your Redguard installation.

Build log

      

    

    
Once your ZIP is ready
  1. Extract it into its own folder, outside your game installation.
  2. On Windows, double-click redguard-native.exe. On Linux, run ./redguard-native in a terminal; it needs a working OpenGL driver.
  3. It looks for your Redguard folder (the one with RGFX.EXE) and asks you to choose it only if it cannot find it, then remembers it. Your saves and settings go into the package's separate user-data folder.
Fullscreen, window size and other options are listed in the README. You can also build locally with the Rust command-line tool.

  

  
Independent fan project. Redguard belongs to Bethesda Softworks and ZeniMax.
Related projects: Redguard Preservation (file format and engine documentation), Cyrus (a Rust rebuild of the game) and the Cyrus asset viewer.
